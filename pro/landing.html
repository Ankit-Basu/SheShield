How it works

One walk, night to dawn.

  1. 01

    Set up your circle

    Create an account and add the person you would want told first. It takes about a minute, and it is ready before you need it.

  2. 02

    Plan the way home

    Check the safety map, find a safe space on your route, or request a walking companion for the stretch you would rather not do alone.

  3. 03

    If it feels wrong, tap

    SOS opens instantly, with no sign-in. Call 112 in one tap, and send your exact location over WhatsApp or SMS to anyone you trust.

  4. 04

    Be heard afterwards

    Report what happened with your name or without it, use a template if the words are hard to find, and follow the case until it is closed.

Every feature here does one job: shorten the distance between something feeling wrong and someone being able to help.

About

Built on campus, for campus.

SheShield is a student-built project. It started with a simple observation: when you feel unsafe, you should not have to work out who to call, where you are, or how to explain it. Those answers should already be on your screen.
